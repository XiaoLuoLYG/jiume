# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want JiuMe to be a respectful, practical, and privacy-aware project space. Contributors and maintainers are expected to work with care, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on improving the software and its documentation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Share concrete evidence for bugs, regressions, and product claims.
- Protect private data, credentials, screenshots, chat exports, and local twin state.
- Accept maintainer feedback about scope, privacy, security, and release readiness.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone else's private data or credentials.
- Pressuring contributors to disclose sensitive local runtime details.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block participants when behavior harms the project or its users. Serious security or privacy concerns may be handled privately first.
